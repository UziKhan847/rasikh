UI rules on a vivid backdrop
Text never sits bare on the scene except headings and one-line subtitles, which are white with a deep-colour outline.
Letters always sit on a solid tile: white face, hard 5px bottom edge, soft drop shadow. Translucent cards are gone; locked items stay solid and fade their content instead.
Buttons that carry text use the theme's deep colour so white labels pass AA. The plain accent is kept for icon-only buttons and progress fills.
