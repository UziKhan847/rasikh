Delete your account

This deletes your Qiraah Arabiyyah marks account on this server:

  • your username, display name and (if you added one) recovery email;
  • the mark books you own, with all their sessions, marks and comments;
  • your membership of other people's books. Marks you made in those books stay in them, as part of that reciter's record, but without your name.

Marks stored on your own devices are not affected. You can also delete your account in the app: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account.
