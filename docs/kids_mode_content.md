# Qiraah Arabiyyah — Kids Mode: content and features

Everything Kids Mode teaches and does, with no visual design: no scenes, themes, layouts or styling. Use it as the content inventory for a redesign.

- **Audience:** children learning to read Arabic, from their first letter to reading short sūrahs, in the classical Qāʼidah Nūrāniyyah order.
- **Languages:** English, Arabic, Urdu, French, Spanish.
- **Data:** everything stays on the device. There are no accounts, no ads and no purchases with real money.

---

## 1. Profiles and access

- **Family profiles.** Several children can share one device. Each has a name, an avatar, and a separate copy of all their progress (stars, XP, gems, streak, badges, shop items, review queue).
- **Profile picker.** Choose who is learning when Kids Mode opens. Profiles can be added from the picker.
- **New profile:** a name, plus an avatar from the free set.
- **Parent gate:** a simple sum to solve. It stands in front of leaving Kids Mode, the parent report and grown-up settings. It isn't a security control; it stops a child from wandering out by accident.
- **Kids Mode on/off:** a parent turns it on from the grown-up app, and leaves it through the parent gate.
- **Testing unlock (developers and parents):** tap the avatar 10 times, then enter a code.
  - It opens every stage, theme and avatar so everything can be tried. Nothing is marked done or owned.
  - Extra options while it is on: "Jump to stage" pins the app to any stage, and a frame-time readout appears.

## 2. The learning path (the curriculum)

The path is a list of **stages**, each made of **items** (lessons, check-ups, games, tests).
- Stages unlock in order, and so do the items inside a stage.
- Each item earns 0 to 3 stars, and one star or more clears it.
- Long stages get a cumulative **check-up after every 5 lessons**, plus a **game after every second check-up**.
- Most stages end on a **Test**. It is the stage's own checks with the teaching cards taken away, worded for children.

### Stage list, in order

| # | Stage | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Letters** (ا ب) | One lesson per letter for all 28 letters, in the classical order alif to yāʼ. Check-ups after letters 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 (each covers every letter so far). Games after letters 10 and 20 (Memory Match, then Three in a Row). |
| 2 | **Letters Quiz** | A cumulative check over the whole alphabet: shape to name and name to shape, with each letter's look-alikes as the wrong options. |
| 3 | **Joining Shapes** (ـبـ) | One lesson per real joining shape: start, middle and end for a letter that joins both ways. A non-joiner (ا و د ذ ر ز) gets only the end form. That makes 79 lessons. Check-ups every 5 letters; games every 10 letters (Three in a Row, then Bingo). |
| 4 | **Shapes Quiz** | A cumulative check over every joining shape: shape to letter, and letter plus position to shape. |
| 5 | **Join the Letters** (بيت) | Drag letters together to build real words, each shown with a picture of what it means (no translation to read). |
| 6 | **Special Shapes** (لا) | Four lessons: ligatures (lām-alif and others), how kāf wraps, letters that stack, and letter heights and tails. Then "meet this character" cards for hamzah (ء), the hamzah seats (أ إ ؤ ئ) and alif maqṣūrah (ى). |
| 7 | **Special Shapes Quiz** | A recognition check over the special shapes and the extra characters. |
| 8 | **Ḥarakāt: fatḥah, kasrah, ḍammah** (بَ) | Syllable drills, one mark at a time, through the alphabet (بَ تَ ثَ …). Each mark is split into short sessions. Then *Practise with words* (real muṣḥaf words carrying the marks), a Bingo game, and the Test. |
| 9 | **Sukūn** (بۡ) | Syllable drills that show the silent letter after each of the three vowels (أَبۡ إِبۡ أُبۡ). Then word practice, a Memory Match game, and the Test (spot the mark). |
| 10 | **Shaddah** (بّ) | Drills using the classical "shaddah with the six ḥarakāt" table on a doubled letter. Then word practice, a Three in a Row game, and the Test. |
| 11 | **Madd and Līn** (بَا) | One stretching mark at a time, each with a beat counter fixed to its own count. Test: "how many counts?" on real words, and "which word is stretched longest?" |
| 12 | **Tanwīn** (بً) | Drills for fatḥatān, kasratān and ḍammatān. *Tanwīn Shapes* teaches that the way tanwīn is drawn (three shapes) tells the reader what to do, then has the child sort real words into the right shape's bin. Then a Bingo game and the Test. |
| 13 | **Ḥurūf Muqaṭṭaʿāt** (الٓمٓ) | How to read the disjointed letters at the start of sūrahs, which needs the madd counts learned earlier. Graded lesson, then Test. |
| 14 | **Reading Words** (قُلۡ) | Reading whole words. Graded lesson, then Test. |
| 15 | **Waṣl (joining)** (ٱ) | Hamzat al-waṣl and carrying the sound from one word into the next. Graded lesson, then Test. |
| 16 | **Sun and Moon Letters** (ال) | When the lām of "al-" is heard and when it hides; lām of the word Allāh. Graded lesson, then Test. |
| 17 | **Tāʼ Marbūṭah** (ة) | A "meet this character" card (the ending that changes when you stop), then a Test. |
| 18 | **Waqf (stopping)** (صلى) | How to stop on a word, and the stopping signs of the muṣḥaf. Graded lesson, then Test. |
| 19 | **Two Words** (٢) | Read Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ two words at a time: read it aloud, then tap. |
| 20 | **Three Words** (٣) | Read Sūrat al-Kawthar three words at a time. |
| 21 | **Tajwīd: Madd** | Rule-by-rule lesson on the madd family, then a Test drawn from every rule in it. |
| 22 | **Tajwīd: Mīm Sākinah** (مْ) | Rule-by-rule lesson, then Test. |
| 23 | **Tajwīd: Nūn Sākinah and Tanwīn** (نْ) | Rule-by-rule lesson (iẓhār, idghām, iqlāb, ikhfāʼ), then Test. |
| 24 | **Tajwīd: Qalqalah** (قْ) | Rule lesson, then Test. |
| 25 | **Tajwīd: Rāʼ** (ر) | Heavy and light rāʼ: rule lesson, then Test. |
| 26 | **Qāʼidah Review** (۝) | A "look how far you've come" recap of every stage cleared, then a review Test over the whole Qāʼidah. |
| 27–42 | **Reading ladder** | One stage per sūrah, from az-Zalzalah (99) to an-Nās (114), in muṣḥaf order. 99–102 and 104 are read three words to a line. Al-ʿAṣr (103) and al-Fīl (105) to an-Nās are read a whole āyah to a line. |

### What each kind of lesson does

- **Letter lesson** (The Letters, Joining Shapes):
  1. Meet the shape.
  2. Hear it and say it out loud.
  3. Trace it 5 times in a row in fewer, longer strokes, with stroke order and direction. The mark is the best of the five.
  4. "Great job!"
  - In Joining Shapes, a non-joiner's lesson opens with a contrast card against the shape the child already knows.
- **Syllable drill** (Ḥarakāt, Sukūn, Shaddah, Tanwīn): a card per syllable with the letter and its mark. The child hears it, says it, and sees a Latin-letter hint for the sound. Short sessions walk the alphabet (all letters except alif).
- **Practise with words** (tamrīn): real muṣḥaf words that carry the mark just drilled, read one at a time. The mark is picked out in colour and its recording is a tap away. It isn't graded; reading every word earns 3 stars.
- **Graded lesson** (Muqaṭṭaʿāt, Words, Waṣl, Sun and Moon, Waqf, Tajwīd): teaching cards with check-ups between them.
- **Test:** the same lesson's checks with the teaching cards removed, or a short quiz worded for children.
- **Check-up:** a cumulative quiz over every letter or shape covered so far, with look-alikes as the wrong options.
- **"Meet this character" card:** one character with one real example, cleared on open.
- **Reading screen:** real Qurʼānic text from the bundled muṣḥaf. "Read it aloud, then tap" moves through one chunk at a time (2 or 3 words, or a whole āyah).
- **Review recap:** a celebration of the stages cleared, with nothing new to learn.

### How checks behave (all quizzes)

- **A second try before a miss counts.** After a first wrong pick, that option is disabled and a pointing hint appears ("look at the small sign above the word"). A second miss reveals the answer. A two-option check reveals at once.
- **Feedback explains why.** Where an explanation exists, both right and wrong answers end on a short explanation the child dismisses with Next.
- **Stars come from first-try answers only,** so a second try can't inflate them.
- **Every miss joins the review queue straight away** (see Warm-up).
- **Ordering tasks** (put words, āyāt or letters in order) can be dragged or tapped. A wrong tile costs the first-try mark but never undoes what has been built. Some rounds include decoy tiles.
- **Sorting tasks:** drag an item into its bin, or tap the item and then the bin.

## 3. Practice (on demand, never blocks progress)

Practice tiles on Home appear as the child learns enough letters for them to make sense. None of them is part of the path.

| Practice | Appears when | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | Something is due for review | Spaced-repetition review of past misses (Leitner boxes): a miss goes back to "due now", and each right answer pushes the next review further out. The hardest and oldest items come first. |
| **Flashcards** | Always | Flip cards over every letter learned so far: tap to reveal the name and an example word. |
| **Listening** | Always | Hear and recognise the letters learned so far. |
| **Look-alikes** | At least one pair of easily confused letters is known | Interleaved "which is different?" practice on letters that differ only in their dots. |
| **Write it** | 1 letter known | Write 5 known letters from memory on a blank board. |
| **Find it** | 1 letter known | Find the word that contains a given letter in a real line of a short sūrah. |
| **Memorise** | 1 letter known | Memorise Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ in three sittings: āyāt 1–2, āyāt 3–4, then the whole sūrah together. Phrases of 2 to 4 words, with a vanishing cue (fewer words shown each step). The revision repeats. |
| **Mouth Map** | 6 letters known | Where sounds come from (makhārij): the five places from the chest to the lips, plus the nose for ghunnah. Plain names ("The Throat", "Open mouth"), the letters that live in each place, and a "feel it" instruction ("put a finger on your throat…"). |
| **Listen and Pick** | 10 letters known | Hear a word, pick it. Rounds of several words. |
| **Build the Word** | 20 letters known | Build words from their letters. |
| **Three in a Row** | 6 letters known | Game (see below) |
| **Memory Match** | 6 letters known | Game (see below) |
| **Letter Bingo** | 10 letters known | Game (see below) |

## 4. Games

All three games use only letters the child has learned. They appear on the path between check-ups and as practice tiles.

- **Three in a Row** (noughts and crosses against the computer).
  - Each of the 9 squares shows a known letter. Tapping a square asks "which letter is this?", and only a right answer claims it.
  - A wrong answer passes the turn without claiming anything.
  - The computer picks a random empty square on purpose: it is reading practice, not a hard opponent.
- **Memory Match.** Find pairs, each made of a letter's shape and its name.
- **Letter Bingo.** A caller says a letter out loud, and the child taps the cell that shows it. Four in a row (row, column or diagonal) wins.
  - Names are never printed, so it trains the ear.
  - There is one guess per call. A miss counts against the letter that was called, and "Listen again" replays the call.

## 5. Rewards and motivation

### XP and levels
- **10 XP per star** earned. A lesson cleared on open (a "meet" card or a recap) gives 10 XP.
- **Level** = 1 + XP ÷ 100.

### Gems (earned only; nothing can be bought with money)
- **5 gems** per completed lesson.
- **+10 gems** when every step of a lesson earned 3 stars.
- **15 gems** for each badge, once, when it unlocks.

### Daily goal and streaks
- **Daily goal:** 5 lessons a day, shown as a ring.
- **When the goal is met,** a calm "this is a good place to stop" message appears. Nothing locks, there is no "one more" push, and there is no streak-anxiety wording.
- **Streak:** the number of days in a row with learning.
- **Week goal:** 5 days a week.
- **Streak freeze:** bought with gems.
  - When it saves the streak, a "Your streak was saved!" banner appears.
  - The first save also unlocks the **Ice Cube avatar**, which is never sold.

### Badges (44)

**Path:**
- First letter; 5 letters; 10 letters; halfway through the alphabet; the whole alphabet.
- All joining shapes written; joining-shapes quiz passed; Special Shapes.
- Ḥarakāt, Sukūn, Shaddah, Madd, Tanwīn and Muqaṭṭaʿāt cleared.
- Reading Words, Waṣl, Sun and Moon Letters, Tāʼ Marbūṭah and Waqf cleared.
- Two Words, Three Words.
- Reached Tajwīd; Mīm, Nūn, Qalqalah and Rāʼ cleared.
- Path finished (Qāʼidah Review passed).
- Read 1, 5, 10 and all 16 sūrahs.

**Habit:**
- 3-, 7-, 14- and 30-day streaks.
- A full week (the week goal met).
- 5 lessons in one day.

**Mastery:**
- First quiz passed; a perfect quiz; 10 perfect results.
- 50 stars; 100 stars.
- Level 5; Level 10.

## 7. For parents

**Parent report** (behind the parent gate):
- The stage the child is on, and lessons done out of the total.
- Level and streak.
- **"Worth practising together":** what is waiting in the Warm-up.
- **"Try this together":** one conversation prompt matched to the kind of stage:
  - *Shapes:* point to the letter, name it, trace it in the air, and say what makes it different from its look-alikes.
  - *Sounds:* say each sound back, swap the vowel, and check they hear the change.
  - *Rules:* have the child explain the rule as if you were the student.
  - *Reading:* the child reads one line slowly, pointing at each word, then you read it back together.
  - *General:* the child teaches you one thing from today's lesson.

**Session reminder:** off, 15, 20 or 30 minutes. It gently suggests a stretch break, which the child can dismiss with "Keep going". It never locks the app.

**Other parent settings:**
- **Backup and restore** of progress.
- **Offline downloads** for audio.
- A note that nothing is collected or sent.

## 8. Audio

- Recordings of letter names and sounds, syllables, words and Qurʼān recitation, word by word and āyah by āyah.
- Letter recordings may be missing. Every activity still works silently and plays the audio once the clips exist.
- Audio is always optional and never required to answer.

## 9. Rules the content follows

- **Fully kid-native.** No Kids Mode tile opens a grown-up page, and no Test asks a grown-up question.
- **Order.** The classical alif-to-yāʼ order is used for teaching. Look-alikes are mixed together in practice only.
- **Answers.** Review cards never show the answer on their face.
- **Qurʼānic text** always comes verbatim from the bundled muṣḥaf and is never retyped.
- **Gems** have no new earning routes beyond those listed above, to avoid rewarding activity for its own sake.
- **Practice never gates progress.** A practice activity is never a path stage.
