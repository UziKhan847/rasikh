Privacy Policy — Qiraah Arabiyyah

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Qiraah Arabiyyah ("the app") does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. This page explains exactly what the app does and doesn't do.

No accounts, no personal data collection

The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information. Nothing you do inside the app is sent to us or to any third party for collection.

What's stored, and where

Your progress, stars, streak, settings, and mistake-review queue are stored only on your own device, using local on-device storage. If you choose to download Qur'an recitation audio for offline use (see below), those audio files are also saved only on your device. None of this data ever leaves your device or is visible to us in any form. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app also lets you back up your progress to a single file saved in the app's own local storage, and restore from it later. This backup file is created and read only on your device — the app never uploads it anywhere. If you copy or share that file yourself (for example, moving it to a new phone), that's your own action, outside the app.

Downloading Qur'an audio for offline use

The app can stream recitation audio on demand, and also lets you download it in advance so it plays without an internet connection. Whether streamed or downloaded, audio playback works the same way: your device requests the relevant file directly from the reciter's hosting service, listed below. We don't operate any of these services ourselves.

Separately, for recitation sourced from the Quran.com/Quran Foundation service (see below), the app keeps a small local log of when each file was last downloaded, purely on your device. This is used to automatically refresh that cached audio if it's more than a week old, per that service's own terms for offline-cached content — it does not involve any personal data, and nothing is sent anywhere except the same routine download request described above.

Network requests

To load Qur'anic recitation audio (streamed or downloaded), your device makes requests to the following third-party services, which — as a normal, unavoidable part of how the internet works — receive your IP address and basic request metadata (not your name or any personal profile):

  • everyayah.com — verse-by-verse recitation by Shaykh Khalīfah Al-Tunaiji, Mishary Al-ʿAfāsī, and Maḥmūd Al-Ḥuṣarī
  • Quran.com / the Quran Foundation's QDC API (api.quran.com, verses.quran.com, and its media mirrors such as quranicaudio.com) — word-timed recitation and audio for Al-ʿAfāsī and Al-Ḥuṣarī
  • audio.qurancdn.com (QuranCDN) — word-by-word recitation audio, courtesy of Markaz Tafsir

We do not control these third parties' own data practices; we chose them because they are established providers of Qur'anic recitation. No word meanings or translations are fetched or shown anywhere in the app.

Every typeface the app uses (for Qur'anic script, tajwīd symbols, and the interface itself) is bundled inside the app install — none is ever downloaded over the network. The app works fully offline for any content you've already viewed or downloaded.

Kids Mode

Kids Mode is an optional, simplified presentation of the app for children — its own design, its own progression (XP, levels, gems, badges), and a household leaderboard. It is not a separate app or account system: a child's profile (a name typed in, a chosen avatar, and — only if given — whether to address them as a boy or girl, so praise messages use the right grammatical form) is stored the exact same way as everything else in this policy — only on your own device. There is no sign-up, no server, and nothing about any child profile is ever collected by us or sent anywhere. A "family leaderboard" only ever compares profiles already on that same device; it does not talk to a network of any kind. Turning Kids Mode on or off, and switching between profiles, are both local settings changes, covered by the backup/restore feature described above like any other progress on the device.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics services, and no tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal data from any user — this is also true inside the optional Kids Mode described above, whose child profiles never leave the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will change accordingly.
