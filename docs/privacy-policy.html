Privacy Policy — Qiraah Arabiyyah

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Qiraah Arabiyyah ("the app") does not sell any data, and collects none unless you choose to create a marks account. This page explains exactly what the app does and doesn't do.

Using the app without an account

Everything in the app works without signing in, and the app does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information to do so. Without an account, nothing you do inside the app is sent to us or to any third party.

Optional marks account (Hifz)

In the Hifz section a listener can mark words of a recitation (a mistake, a tajwīd point, a hesitation, a doubt, a comment). These marks are kept on your device. If you choose to create an account, you can sync a mark book to our server so that a teacher, a listener or a parent can see and add to it from their own device.

What the account holds: a username you choose, a password (stored only as a secure hash), an optional display name, and — only if you add one — a recovery email used for nothing but resetting a forgotten password.

What syncs: only the mark books you choose to sync, and the books others have shared with you: their names, recitation sessions (date, kind, the range read, the listener's name you typed, a note), the marks (sūrah, āyah and word; the type of mark; comments), when marks were fixed, and who made each mark (username / display name). Your other progress, stars, streak, settings and backups never leave your device.

Who can see it: the owner of a mark book, and the people the owner invites with a share code (listeners, who can mark, and checkers, who can read and comment). The owner can remove anyone at any time. Usernames are not public.

Where it's stored: on a server we run ourselves (open-source PocketBase), hosted by [VPS provider] in [country/region], at [marks.example.com]. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). If you add a recovery email, password-reset emails are sent through [email provider]. No one else receives your data, and it is never used for advertising or analytics.

Keeping and deleting it: synced data stays until you delete it. Deleted marks are removed from the server within 90 days. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account & sync → Delete account) or at [https://marks.example.com/delete-account.html]. This deletes your account and the mark books you own, with everything in them. Marks you made in someone else's book stay in that book as part of that reciter's record, but without your name. Data on your own devices is not affected. Server backups are kept for 7 days.

What's stored, and where

Your progress, stars, streak, settings, and mistake-review queue are stored only on your own device, using local on-device storage. So are your Hifz marks, unless you sync a mark book as described above. If you choose to download Qur'an recitation audio for offline use (see below), those audio files are also saved only on your device. None of this data ever leaves your device or is visible to us in any form. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app also lets you back up your progress and marks to a single file saved in the app's own local storage, and restore from it later. This backup file is created and read only on your device — the app never uploads it anywhere. If you copy or share that file yourself (for example, moving it to a new phone), that's your own action, outside the app.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the app asks your device for permission to show notifications and schedules one local notification a day at the time you choose. The reminder is created and shown entirely by your device — nothing is sent over the network, and no one else learns that you set it or when. Turning the reminder off (or uninstalling the app) cancels it.

Downloading Qur'an audio for offline use

The app can stream recitation audio on demand, and also lets you download it in advance so it plays without an internet connection. Whether streamed or downloaded, audio playback works the same way: your device requests the relevant file directly from the reciter's hosting service, listed below. We don't operate any of these services ourselves.

Separately, for recitation sourced from the Quran.com/Quran Foundation service (see below), the app keeps a small local log of when each file was last downloaded, purely on your device. This is used to automatically refresh that cached audio if it's more than a week old, per that service's own terms for offline-cached content — it does not involve any personal data, and nothing is sent anywhere except the same routine download request described above.

Network requests

To load Qur'anic recitation audio (streamed or downloaded), your device makes requests to the following third-party services, which — as a normal, unavoidable part of how the internet works — receive your IP address and basic request metadata (not your name or any personal profile):

  • everyayah.com — verse-by-verse recitation by Shaykh Khalīfah Al-Tunaiji, Mishary Al-ʿAfāsī, and Maḥmūd Al-Ḥuṣarī
  • Quran.com / the Quran Foundation's QDC API (api.quran.com, verses.quran.com, and its media mirrors such as quranicaudio.com) — word-timed recitation and audio for Al-ʿAfāsī and Al-Ḥuṣarī
  • audio.qurancdn.com (QuranCDN) — word-by-word recitation audio, courtesy of Markaz Tafsir

If you sign in to a marks account, the app also talks to our own marks server (see Optional marks account above) — and only then.

We do not control these third parties' own data practices; we chose them because they are established providers of Qur'anic recitation. No word meanings or translations are fetched or shown anywhere in the app.

Every typeface the app uses (for Qur'anic script, tajwīd symbols, and the interface itself) is bundled inside the app install — none is ever downloaded over the network. The app works fully offline for any content you've already viewed or downloaded.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics services, and no tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

Children don't need, and shouldn't create, their own marks account. A child's mark book is meant to be owned by a parent or teacher's account, and the reciter's name on it can be a first name or a nickname. Without an account the app collects no data from anyone, children included.
